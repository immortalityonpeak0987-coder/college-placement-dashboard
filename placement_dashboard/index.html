The takeaway for a junior student

Before chasing a big package, prepare for the companies that show up at almost every college.

    What to do: Pick the top 5 recurring companies, study their past interview patterns (aptitude, basic coding, communication), and start a little daily practice early in your degree. That preparation pays off in almost every college's placement drive.

    Method and limitations

    Method

    • Removed repeated header rows and duplicate records.
    • Normalised company, college and region text.
    • Converted packages (yearly and monthly) to LPA.
    • Merged inconsistent company spellings after manual review.
