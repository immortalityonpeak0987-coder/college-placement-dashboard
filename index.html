Known Limitations & Analytical Guardrails

Explicit boundaries enforced throughout this dashboard to ensure honest reporting.

1. Records ≠ Unique Students Placed

Each record represents a company recruiting at a given college in a given year. The dataset does not contain student roll numbers, hiring headcounts, or total eligible student populations.

2. Partial Salary Disclosure (14.51%)

Only 399 of 2,749 records include verified package values from variation_3.csv. Unreported packages are kept as missing (null) and never imputed or zero-filled.

3. No Branch / Department Column

The underlying source files do not record engineering branch (e.g., CSE, IT, ECE, Mechanical). All breakdowns are strictly limited to verified region, college, company, and year dimensions.
